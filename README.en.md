### About

Since 2016 I have been building cross-platform products and the architecture around them. Day to day that is Flutter, Android, React Native, and Swift: Clean Architecture, DDD, decisions written in Design Docs and ADRs, and legacy migrated without stopping the app.

Around that I look after observability, CI/CD with quality gates, and backend in NestJS, TypeScript, and PostgreSQL on AWS. I also work with AI agents in spec-driven development, and with LLM solutions — orchestration, RAG, evals, and guardrails. Off the clock, I learn by shipping my own products.
